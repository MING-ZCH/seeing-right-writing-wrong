Two controls test whether the cost of written reasoning reflects a ceiling effect or model scale. On a harder version of the occlusion twins (thin overlap, low contrast, distractor shapes, blur), Direct accuracy falls to 85.2% for Claude Opus 5.5 and 91.3% for GPT-6-Astra. Written reasoning leaves their sensitivity unchanged or higher (d′ 2.08 to 2.04; 2.72 to 2.98), and the result holds with hidden reasoning set to the lowest available level. Within one open family, Qwen3-VL Instruct, written reasoning lowers d′ at every size: by 0.44 (2B), 0.93 (4B), 0.46 (8B) and 1.96 (32B). The 32B model has the highest Direct sensitivity among the open models (4.47) and the largest loss. Qwen3.8-Max shows a smaller loss (1.76 without reasoning, 1.61 with written reasoning; the interval includes zero). Open models are near chance on the harder set, so the two groups are compared at similar Direct sensitivity across sets.
